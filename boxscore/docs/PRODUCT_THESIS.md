# Product Thesis

GPs and asset managers pay for Boxscore because diagnosing NOI misses is slow, messy, and often trapped in scattered exports, inbox explanations, and staff memory.

Large institutions care because they need consistent operating accountability across managers, regions, markets, and time. A lender, allocator, or owner does not only want a memo; they want an evidence trail that explains what changed and what is still unknown.

The moat is not the model. Models get cheaper and more interchangeable.

The moat is:

- Multifamily ontology.
- Normalized operating data.
- Evidence-backed claims.
- Workflow memory.
- Gap history.
- Operator feedback loops.
- Repeated capability proposals grounded in real usage.

Potential product line:

- Boxscore Ops: monthly and weekly operating variance intelligence.
- Boxscore Capital: capex, reserves, debt, cash, and covenant intelligence.
- Boxscore Acquisitions: diligence, underwriting variance, and operating-risk discovery.
- Boxscore Investor Reporting: evidence-backed owner and LP reporting.
- Boxscore Lender Surveillance: portfolio monitoring for lenders and servicers.
